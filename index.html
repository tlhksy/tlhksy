Orbit · Solar geometry

Overpass Planner

SAT Sentinel-2 PASS 12:09 local SOLAR 10:52 TST ZENITH 26.9°

When will a satellite pass over my field site, and where will the sun be at that moment? Click a point on the map; the page fetches current orbital elements, propagates them with SGP4, and returns overpass times for Sentinel-2, Landsat, Sentinel-1 and MODIS — each with the ground track, a swath-edge warning, and the solar elevation, zenith and true solar time at closest approach. Built to align UAV flights with satellite acquisitions.

↗ 02

Vector data · GIS ⇄ CAD

Pafta — vector converter

IN SHP · GeoJSON · KML · GPX OUT DXF · SHP · KML · CSV CRS ≤ 1 mm vs PROJ

A converter between GIS and CAD formats — but the point is not the conversion, it is the loss report. Every run says exactly what it gave up: field names truncated to ten characters, layers transliterated to ASCII, polygon holes split into separate polylines. It refuses to write a DXF in geographic coordinates, because a unitless drawing measured in degrees is silently broken. Transforms agree with PROJ to better than a millimetre; outputs are checked in CI against pyshp and ezdxf — third-party readers, not its own. Pafta means map sheet.

↗ 03

Field survey · Transect

Field Visibility Recorder

STOP photo + class SENSOR azimuth · bearing · slope OUT PDF · GeoJSON · CSV

A phone-first tool for walking a transect. At each stop you photograph and classify the view, and the app records position, camera azimuth, route bearing, GPS elevation and segment slope. A built-in clinometer measures ground slope and object height from the phone's orientation sensors. Everything is stored on the device and resumes where you left off; export as a printable site report, GeoJSON for GIS and Earth Engine, or a CSV attribute table.
